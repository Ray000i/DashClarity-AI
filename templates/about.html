System Architecture & Research Context

Technical documentation for DashClarity AI — An AI-powered dashcam footage enhancement platform utilizing Spatial-Temporal Consistent Decomposition (STCD).

1. Problem Statement & Motivation

Dashcam video surveillance is critical for traffic accident reconstruction, hit-and-run liability attribution, and automotive insurance claims verification. However, night-time dashcam footage frequently suffers from:

  • Extreme Low Illumination: Unlit rural roads and poorly lit urban intersections provide insufficient photons to CMOS image sensors.
  • Heavy Sensor Noise & Artifacts: High ISO sensor gains introduce non-Gaussian chromatic grain and motion blur.
  • Temporal Inconsistency & Flickering: Applying naive single-frame image enhancement models (such as RetinexNet or standard GANs) independently to each video frame produces intolerable visual flicker and unstable brightness fluctuations across consecutive frames.

2. Mathematical Formulation & Retinex Theory

DashClarity AI incorporates the Spatial-Temporal Consistent Decomposition (STCD) framework presented at IJCAI 2025. According to classical Retinex Theory, a low-light frame \(I\) can be decomposed into reflectance \(R\) and illumination \(L\):

\(I(x, y, t) = R(x, y, t) \odot L(x, y, t)\)

In video sequences, \(R\) represents the view-independent intrinsic appearance of the physical objects (vehicles, road surface, traffic signs), while \(L\) describes the view-dependent dynamic shading and lighting (headlights, streetlamps).

STCD enforces two complementary temporal constraints:

  1. Cross-Frame Intrinsic Correspondence: Leverages dynamic correspondence between frame \(I_t\) and neighbor \(I_{t+1}\) to align the reflectance component \(R\), ensuring object colors and edges remain constant over time.
  2. Scene-Level Illumination Smoothness: Enforces smooth spatio-temporal continuity on the illumination map \(L\), completely preventing artificial high-frequency brightness oscillations between frames.

5. Real-World Applications

Automotive Insurance Claims

Provides claims adjusters with clear visualization of intersection dynamics, lane positions, and traffic signals during night collisions that would otherwise be rejected as inconclusive.

Fleet Safety & Incident Investigation

Fleet management teams can audit driver behavior, near-miss events, and roadway hazards occurring during late-night haulage shifts.

6. Limitations & Academic References

Model Limitations: STCD enhances visibility and suppresses temporal flicker, but cannot hallucinate sub-pixel details (e.g. completely blurred license plates at distance) that were physically absent from the CMOS sensor's captured photons. Severe lens glare from high-beam headlights may create localized haloing.
