System limits and architecture

Technical briefing on model stages, hardware execution benchmarks, physical failure modes, and evidentiary boundaries.

Pipeline stages
1. Sensor denoising (SCUNet)
Deep residual Swin-Conv denoiser removes CMOS Poisson-Gaussian noise variance and compression block artifacts before illumination boost to avoid amplifying sensor grain.
2. Illumination boost (Zero-DCE++)
Estimates dynamic high-order non-linear tone curves without paired reference images. Highlight knee compression protects oncoming headlights from blooming.
3. Detail reconstruction (Real-ESRGAN)
RRDBNet generator synthesizes fine edge gradients. Blends 22% Lanczos reference interpolation to mitigate GAN ringing and halo artifacts around text.
4. Temporal consistency (Dense Farneback flow)
Estimates inter-frame camera motion vectors to warp prior enhanced frames forward, eliminating inter-frame flicker and ghosting trails across moving roads.
Execution hardware & benchmarks
Hardware architecture
Inference runs locally using PyTorch with CUDA acceleration and adaptive VRAM profiling for 6 GB (RTX 4050) and 8 GB (RTX 4060) mobile GPUs (measured on NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU, 8 GB VRAM).
Memory envelope
Models execute in half-precision (FP16, channels-last) with memory-aware untiled inference and adaptive tiling to keep measured peak allocated VRAM under 1.49 GB at 720p and under 3.1 GB at 1080p (within 6 GB RTX 4050 and 8 GB RTX 4060 envelopes).
Measured throughput (RTX 4060)
Benchmarked in FP16 at 720p: Fast preset at 26.83 FPS (587.8 MB VRAM), Balanced preset at 3.78 FPS (675.3 MB VRAM), and Quality full pipeline at 1.24 FPS (1484.3 MB VRAM).
Known limitations & boundary conditions
Direct headlight glare
Pixels where the camera CMOS sensor has completely clipped to pure white contain zero underlying spatial information. Deep learning models cannot recover license plates concealed directly within a saturated high-beam glare cone.
Severe motion blur
High-speed crossing vehicles that smear across multiple pixels due to slow dashcam exposure times cannot be deblurred if optical flow tracking loses temporal correspondence.
Distant unreadable characters
When license plate alphanumeric characters occupy fewer than 6 to 8 pixels, generative super-resolution will synthesize plausible character strokes, but these strokes may not match the vehicle's true registration string.
Ethics & legal admissibility
Investigative intent
DashClarity AI is intended solely for investigative review, internal fleet claims triage, and preliminary incident inspection.
Evidentiary rules
Under legal rules of evidence, footage processed with generative deep learning is not original recorded evidence. AI upscaling can hallucinate synthetic edge details that were absent in the raw physical recording.
Audit trail requirement
Original raw footage must always be archived alongside enhanced outputs with synchronized comparison logs and objective schema metrics.
