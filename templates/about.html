System limits and architecture

Technical briefing on model stages, hardware execution, physical failure modes, and evidentiary boundaries.

Pipeline stages

Sensor denoising: SCUNet neural denoiser loaded via Spandrel removes CMOS noise variance and compression block artifacts before illumination boost to avoid amplifying sensor grain.

Illumination boost: Zero-DCE++ estimates dynamic high-order non-linear tone curves without paired reference images. Highlight knee compression protects oncoming headlights from blooming.

Detail reconstruction: Real-ESRGAN synthesizes fine edge gradients. Blends twenty-two percent Lanczos reference interpolation to mitigate GAN ringing and halo artifacts around text.

Temporal consistency: Dense Farneback optical flow estimates inter-frame camera motion vectors to warp prior enhanced frames forward, avoiding ghosting trails across moving roads.

Execution hardware

Inference runs locally using PyTorch with CUDA acceleration on dedicated laptop hardware (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 Laptop GPU, 8 GB VRAM).

Models execute in half-precision (FP16) tensor format with spatial tiling to keep peak VRAM under 1.5 GB during 720p and 1080p processing.

Known limitations and boundary conditions

Direct headlight glare: Pixels where the camera CMOS sensor has completely clipped to pure white contain zero underlying spatial information. Deep learning models cannot recover license plates concealed directly within a saturated high-beam glare cone.

Severe motion blur: High-speed crossing vehicles that smear across multiple pixels due to slow dashcam exposure times cannot be deblurred if optical flow tracking loses temporal correspondence.

Distant unreadable characters: When license plate alphanumeric characters occupy fewer than 6 to 8 pixels, generative super-resolution will synthesize plausible character strokes, but these strokes may not match the vehicle's true registration string.

Ethics and legal admissibility

DashClarity AI is intended solely for investigative review, internal fleet claims triage, and preliminary incident inspection.

Under legal rules of evidence, footage processed with generative deep learning is not original recorded evidence. AI upscaling can hallucinate synthetic edge details that were absent in the raw physical recording.
